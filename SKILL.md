## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design reinterprets the subject through a cream aesthetic. It is not a cream model of the real object. Soften the contour, simplify the structure, round the form and allow a slight melt, asymmetry and surreal drift. Keep the subject very small, centered or slightly shifted, inside a minimal cream geometric field with a soft lift. Reject a placed product shot, hard plastic, a filled scene and a literal food replica.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.

Write every selected final PNG directly inside one fresh task directory under `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-panel-258/` (or the explicit `--out` root). Use collision-safe filenames; do not create source, mode, or size subdirectories and do not generate an automatic contact sheet.
